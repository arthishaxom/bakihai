# Any Member may append Entries; Settlements are claimed then confirmed

Device keys sign every Entry, so authorship is provable, but consent is not: any Member may append an Expense, Loan, Return, or Void involving anyone in the Group, and any Member may Void any Entry — the Void records who did it. A Settlement is appended by the payer as a claim and counts toward the Balance immediately, marked unconfirmed until the receiver records a confirmation or Voids it.

## Considered Options

- **A Member may only append Entries that move their own Balance** — impossible for a shared book: "Rohan took 3 eggs from You" is recorded by either of them.
- **Settlements count only once confirmed** — safer against a false claim, but stalls Balances behind the receiver's phone and turns offline confirmation into a blocker.
- **No confirmation at all** — a disputed payment becomes invisible; the two-sided confirm mirrors how the group already describes UPI payments in chat.

## Consequences

Correctness rests on social trust plus visible history; disputes are resolved by Void, not by protocol.
